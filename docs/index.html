How does RF Signal Detector find hidden cameras?

It combines three methods that phones are genuinely good at: a Bluetooth scan that lists every Bluetooth device and tracker around you, a Wi-Fi network scan that lists every device connected to the local network, and an infrared lens finder that uses your iPhone camera to spot hidden lenses. A guided sweep checklist then walks you through the spots devices are most often hidden.

Is RF Signal Detector free?

The app is free to download from the App Store and comes with a free trial, so you can run a full sweep of a room before paying anything.

Does the app work in an Airbnb or hotel?

Yes. In an Airbnb, the Wi-Fi scan lists every device on the rental's network, including cameras. In hotels, where Wi-Fi is shared, the Bluetooth scan and lens finder do the heavy lifting. The sweep checklist adapts to both.

Can any app detect every hidden device?

No, and you should distrust apps that claim otherwise. Phones cannot see wired cameras on a separate network or cellular bugs.
