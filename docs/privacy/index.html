Privacy policy

Last updated August 9, 2026

This policy covers the rfsignaldetectorapp.com website. The short version: this is a static website that collects no personal data.

What this website collects

Nothing. This site has no accounts, no forms, no analytics scripts, no advertising, and no cookies set by us. Pages are served as static files from a hosting provider (GitHub Pages), which processes standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested page) as any web host does to deliver content. We do not access or store those logs.

The device checker tool

The hidden camera device checker runs entirely in your browser. MAC addresses you paste are checked against a manufacturer list downloaded to your device and are never transmitted to us or to any third party. Closing the page erases everything.

External links

Guides link to external sources and to the App Store. Those destinations have their own privacy policies. The RF Signal Detector iOS app has its own privacy practices, disclosed on its App Store listing before you install.

Changes and contact

If our practices ever change, this page changes first and the date above is updated. Questions: baidovich800122@gmail.com.
